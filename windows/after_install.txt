DeepWave is installed.

FL STUDIO
  1. Open FL Studio (version 20.8 or newer, 64-bit).
  2. Options > Manage plugins > "Find installed plugins" (a rescan).
  3. Add > Instruments > DeepWave   (it is under "Installed > Generators").
  Play it from the piano roll, a MIDI keyboard, or the on-screen grid in the plugin window.

OTHER HOSTS (Ableton Live, Reaper, Cubase, Bitwig, Studio One)
  Rescan plugins; DeepWave is a VST3 instrument.

STANDALONE
  Start menu > DeepWave. Pick your audio device under Options > Audio/MIDI Settings.
  Your computer keyboard plays notes too: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P, Z / X for octave.

To remove DeepWave: Windows Settings > Apps > DeepWave > Uninstall.
